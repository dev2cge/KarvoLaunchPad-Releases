# Launch announcement draft

Meet **Karvo LaunchPad** — your Minecraft launcher, HUD tools and cosmetic locker in one place.

Play supported versions **1.8.9, 1.12.2, 1.21.1 and 26.2**, customise your HUD, keep server-specific layouts and choose which owned emotes appear on your wheel. Armour durability, session statistics and screenshot tools help keep the useful details close at hand.

Launcher downloads are available for **Windows, Linux and macOS**. Minecraft Java Edition ownership is required; optional cosmetics and Karvo+ are sold separately.

Download and release notes: https://github.com/dev2cge/KarvoLaunchPad-Releases/releases/latest
Website: https://karvobot.uk/launchpad/

Windows builds currently use Karvo's self-signed certificate, so Windows may show a publisher or SmartScreen warning. Check the official download source and published checksums.

Tried it? We’d welcome reproducible bug reports and honest feedback.
