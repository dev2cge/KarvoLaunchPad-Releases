# Creator and server pilot

Proposed pilot: invite a small number of Minecraft creators and community servers to test Karvo, report compatibility issues and optionally publish their honest experience. No partnerships are currently announced by this document.

Suitable applicants operate an active, moderated community, can test a supported game version and agree to distinguish personal opinion from measured performance. Do not require a positive review. Any free cosmetics, sponsorship or placement must be disclosed to viewers. No payment, exclusivity or server placement is promised without a separate agreement.

Initial review: community description, public website/channel, supported Minecraft versions, audience fit and moderation/contact route. Do not request passwords, account tokens or private member data. Use public business contact channels only after the owner approves outreach.

Success measures: reproducible bug reports, successful launches, voluntary user feedback and clearly labelled demonstrations. Do not invent user counts or reviews.
